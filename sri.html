Self Reliant India (SRI) Fund

Objective

The Self Reliant India Fund provides growth capital to MSMEs through equity and venture funding support, helping enterprises expand and become globally competitive.

Benefits

  • Equity funding support.
  • Growth capital assistance.
  • Expansion opportunities.
  • Improved competitiveness.
  • Access to professional fund management.

Eligibility

  • Growth-oriented MSMEs.
  • MSMEs with scalable business models.
  • Enterprises meeting fund eligibility criteria.

Documents Required

  • Udyam Registration.
  • PAN Card.
  • Business Plan.
  • Financial Statements.
  • Company Incorporation Documents.
  • Bank Account Details.

Application Process

  1. Approach participating Daughter Funds.
  2. Submit business proposal.
  3. Financial and technical due diligence.
  4. Investment evaluation.
  5. Approval and fund disbursement.

Funding Support

  • Equity investment support.
  • Growth and expansion funding.
  • Support through Daughter Funds.
