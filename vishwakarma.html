PM Vishwakarma

Objective

Support artisans and craftspeople with training, tools, credit and market access.

Benefits

  • Certificate & ID Card.
  • Toolkit incentive up to ₹15,000.
  • Collateral-free loan up to ₹3 lakh.
  • Digital transaction incentives.
  • Marketing support.

Eligibility

Artisans and craftspeople in notified trades.

Documents Required

  • Aadhaar Card
  • PAN Card
  • Mobile Number linked with Aadhaar
  • Bank Account Details
  • Occupation Verification Documents
  • Passport Size Photograph

Application Process

  1. Visit the PM Vishwakarma Portal.
  2. Complete registration through Common Service Centre (CSC).
  3. Verify eligibility and artisan category.
  4. Submit required documents.
  5. Approval by competent authority.
  6. Receive benefits and training support.

Financial Assistance

  • Skill upgradation training.
  • Toolkit incentive of ₹15,000.
  • Collateral-free enterprise loans.
  • Digital transaction incentives.
  • Marketing support for artisans.

Loan Benefits

  • First Loan: Up to ₹1 Lakh.
  • Second Loan: Up to ₹2 Lakhs.
  • Concessional Interest Rate: 5%.
