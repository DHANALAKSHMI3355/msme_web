Tool Rooms and Technical Institutions

Objective

To provide advanced technical training and tooling support to MSMEs.

Benefits

  • Advanced manufacturing training.
  • Tool design support.
  • CAD/CAM facilities.
  • Industry-ready skill development.

Eligibility

Students, entrepreneurs and MSMEs seeking technical support and training.

Documents Required

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Educational Certificates
  • PAN Card
  • Photographs
  • Training Application Form

Application Process

  1. Select desired Tool Room course.
  2. Register online.
  3. Upload documents.
  4. Pay applicable fees.
  5. Join training program.

Financial Assistance

  • Advanced technical training.
  • Skill development support.
  • Industry-oriented certification.
