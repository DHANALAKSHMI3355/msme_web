Entrepreneurship Skill Development Programme (ESDP)

Objective

Promote entrepreneurship and support creation of new MSMEs.

Benefits

  • Entrepreneurship Awareness Programs.
  • Skill Development Training.
  • Management Development Programs.

Eligibility

Aspiring and Existing Entrepreneurs.

Documents Required

  • Aadhaar Card
  • PAN Card
  • Educational Qualification Certificates
  • Passport Size Photographs
  • Address Proof
  • Training Registration Form

Application Process

  1. Select an ESDP training program.
  2. Register through MSME Development Institute.
  3. Submit required documents.
  4. Attend training sessions.
  5. Complete assessments and certification process.

Financial Assistance

  • Entrepreneurship development training.
  • Skill enhancement programs.
  • Business management guidance.
  • Support for self-employment opportunities.
