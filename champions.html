MSME Champions Scheme

Objective

MSME Champions aims to support MSMEs through technology adoption, quality improvement, innovation, and grievance redressal mechanisms.

Benefits

  • Technology upgradation support.
  • Business counseling and mentoring.
  • Grievance resolution assistance.
  • Market access support.
  • Promotion of innovation.

Eligibility

  • Registered MSMEs.
  • Manufacturing and service enterprises.
  • MSMEs seeking modernization and growth.

Documents Required

  • Udyam Registration Certificate.
  • PAN Card.
  • GST Registration.
  • Business Profile.
  • Bank Account Details.

Application Process

  1. Visit MSME Champions Portal.
  2. Register your enterprise.
  3. Submit assistance request.
  4. Upload required documents.
  5. Receive support and guidance.

Project Support

  • Technology support.
  • Innovation assistance.
  • Business mentoring.
  • Capacity building support.
