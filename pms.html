Procurement and Marketing Support (PMS) Scheme

Objective

To enhance market access and competitiveness of MSMEs through exhibitions, trade fairs and buyer-seller meets.

Benefits

  • Participation in national and international exhibitions.
  • Marketing support.
  • Buyer-Seller Meets.
  • Vendor Development Programs.

Eligibility

Registered MSMEs with valid Udyam Registration.

Documents Required

  • Udyam Registration Certificate
  • PAN Card
  • GST Registration
  • Business Details
  • Bank Account Details

Application Process

  1. Register under PMS Scheme.
  2. Submit participation request.
  3. Upload supporting documents.
  4. Approval from MSME authority.
  5. Participate in exhibitions and trade fairs.

Financial Assistance

  • Support for trade fairs.
  • Marketing assistance.
  • Buyer-seller meet participation support.
