RAMP Scheme

Objective

Improve MSME access to finance, technology, innovation and markets.

Benefits

  • Technology Upgradation.
  • Market Access.
  • Credit Support.
  • Digitization Support.
  • Greening Initiatives.

Target Beneficiaries

Over 5.5 lakh MSMEs through State Government agencies.

Documents Required

  • Udyam Registration Certificate
  • PAN Card
  • GST Registration Certificate
  • Business Profile
  • Financial Statements
  • Bank Account Details

Application Process

  1. Check eligibility under RAMP components.
  2. Apply through designated MSME authorities.
  3. Submit business and financial details.
  4. Verification and assessment process.
  5. Approval and implementation support.

Financial Assistance

  • Support for MSME competitiveness.
  • Market access and technology adoption assistance.
  • Capacity building and institutional strengthening.
  • State-level MSME development support.
