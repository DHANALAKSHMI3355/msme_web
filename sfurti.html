SFURTI

Objective

Promote traditional industries and increase artisans' income through sustainable employment.

Benefits

  • Up to ₹2.5 crore support for clusters up to 500 artisans.
  • Up to ₹5 crore support for clusters above 500 artisans.
  • Skill development and marketing support.
  • Design and raw material support.

Eligibility

Artisans involved in Handicrafts, Textile, Agro Processing, Bamboo, Honey, Coir and Khadi sectors.

Documents Required

  • Organization Registration Certificate
  • PAN Card
  • Cluster Development Proposal
  • Detailed Project Report (DPR)
  • Bank Account Details
  • Land/Infrastructure Documents

Application Process

  1. Identify traditional industry cluster.
  2. Prepare Detailed Project Report.
  3. Submit proposal through Implementing Agency.
  4. Technical and financial appraisal.
  5. Approval by Scheme Steering Committee.
  6. Release of funds and project implementation.

Financial Assistance

  • Support for traditional artisan clusters.
  • Infrastructure development assistance.
  • Skill development and capacity building.
  • Market promotion support.

Project Cost Limits

  • Regular Cluster: Up to ₹2.5 Crore.
  • Major Cluster: Up to ₹5 Crore.
