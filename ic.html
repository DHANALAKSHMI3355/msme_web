International Cooperation (IC) Scheme

Objective

To promote international cooperation and expand global opportunities for MSMEs.

Benefits

  • Participation in international exhibitions.
  • Business delegations abroad.
  • Technology transfer opportunities.
  • Global market exposure.

Eligibility

MSMEs, Industry Associations and Export Promotion Organizations.

Documents Required

  • Udyam Registration
  • PAN Card
  • Passport Copies
  • Business Profile
  • Export Related Documents

Application Process

  1. Apply for international event participation.
  2. Submit business profile.
  3. Upload supporting documents.
  4. Authority verification.
  5. Approval and financial support.

Financial Assistance

  • International exhibitions support.
  • Foreign trade delegation assistance.
  • Export promotion activities.
